## Lessons (also in memory)
- Fresh `JM_FIT_MODE=eval` runs only: inside one irgold_fit process the metric is path-dependent by ±0.02.
- Open-loop skidpad slide windows are chaotic. Use the closed-loop judge (`JM_IRG_CLOSED=1`) for anything touching slides.
- The part-throttle map is a fraction between EFRIC and the WOT table: do not refit one without the others.
- Teleport screenshots are not reproducible run to run (pose). Judge render changes geometrically, e.g. `JM_LAPWIN_DIAG`.
- `pkill -f <pattern>` kills your own shell: kill by PID.
