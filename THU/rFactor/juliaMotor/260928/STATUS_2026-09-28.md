## The two big jobs (you said these come first)

### Job A — Make the computer drivers act like GPL's drivers

**This one worked. It is the best result of the session.**

GPL keeps its driver settings in a file on your computer, with notes explaining every number.
I found it (`~/sgl/THU/.../gpl_ai.ini`, plus the real 1967 driver list in `driver.ini`). It
answered your question about why GPL's cars move sideways smoothly and ours jerked.

GPL moves a car sideways like a spring pulling it — smooth and gentle. I had written something
rougher that yanked the car in steps. I replaced mine with GPL's method and used GPL's real
numbers.

| what was measured | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| sideways jerkiness (typical worst) | 216 | **58** |
| sideways jerkiness (very worst) | 480 | **69** |
| time to change lanes | 1.68 s | **1.50 s** |

So it is smoother **and** slightly quicker.

I also found that GPL includes a map of how wide the road is at every point. We were using one
guessed width for the whole track (about 3.8 m each side). GPL's real numbers run from 2.9 m up
to 21 m, and they differ on the left and right. We now use GPL's map. Because of that,
**Watkins Glen now uses GPL's own racing line**, which it refused to do before.

### Job B — Make the tracks look like GPL's tracks

**Partly done, and I made a bad mistake here.**

First I had to build a way to measure the problem. Every measuring tool this project had looked
at whether the road goes *up and down* smoothly. Your complaint is about the road's shape seen
*from above* — the white line at the edge breaking into straight pieces. Nobody had ever
measured that.

Building that tool took five tries. Four gave nonsense. One even gave impossible answers and
still said "PASS", which is worse than having no tool at all. The fifth one works.

It showed the white line bulges away from its true curve by up to **1.15 m** at Watkins Glen.
That is your defect, measured for the first time.

Then I found a single line of code blocking the fix: it refused to smooth any road piece longer
than about 36 m, and the worst pieces are 31–55 m. I changed it.

**Here is my mistake.** I told you that change made things **2.1 times better**. It did not. I
had measured against the wrong reference curve. The real improvement is about **2 % at Watkins
Glen** and **37 % at Spa**. I was off by roughly twentyfold. I found the error myself in the next
sprint and wrote the correction down, but I should not have reported the first number so
confidently.

## The three smaller jobs

### Job 1 — Nürburgring tree curtains, and trees poking into the road

**Found it exactly. Could not fix it.**

There are **40 places** where flat tree pictures stretch across the road. They are named
`half01` through `half09` (plus `half1s`). Some cover the entire road width. Your second
complaint is a different thing — small bushes (`shrub_s`, `shrub_a`) that poke in by at most
**1.25 m**.

Then I tried three fixes and **all three were wrong**:

1. I thought the scenery was built backwards compared to the track. It is not — they match. I
   had to take back what I had written the sprint before.
2. I thought the trees were rotated 90° by mistake. I tested it. Rotating them made things
   **five times worse**, so GPL means them to be that way.
3. I thought we were showing the wrong side of each flat picture. I tested it. The pictures have
   **both** sides built in, so hiding one side cannot work.

Four sprints, a precise diagnosis, three dead ends, no fix. That is an honest failure.

### Job 2 — Watkins Glen's doubled start/finish banner

**Probably solved. Needs one test drive from you.**

There is only **one** banner in the game's files. So we are drawing something twice.

I found why. An older fix in our code (note E58) was meant to remove a leg that stuck into the
road. Instead it deleted the **whole crossbar** — but kept the **logo sign** that hung from that
crossbar, at exactly the same height. So the game now draws a tower, plus a sign floating in the
air where the crossbar used to be. That looks exactly like two banners.

**Please try this:**

```
TRACK=watglen JM_STARTBOX_KEEP=1   (then drive past the start line)
```

If the double banner disappears and nothing blocks the track, I will remove that old fix for
good. If a leg really does stick out, `JM_STARTBOX_PUSH=6` is the other half of E58 — it moves
the banner aside instead of taking it apart.

The yellow shape over the road is the same story: Spa's track shape contains nothing at all
