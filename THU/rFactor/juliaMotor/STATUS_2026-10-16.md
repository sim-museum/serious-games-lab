## What you will notice in the game
1. **Launch is about 10 s faster** (Spa: 147 s → 137 s to the game loop). The physics package was making the renderer
   recompile 41 of its cached methods at every launch. They are now built together, so the cache holds.
2. **Fewer stutters while racing.** The game produced about 190 KB of garbage per frame, causing a 25–35 ms
   hitch roughly every 20 s. Half of it came from one shader handle the compiler could not see the type of. It is
   now about 90 KB per frame, and Spa's hitches dropped from 7 to 4 per 2.5 minutes.
3. **No slowdown after a crash.** Once your car was a frozen wreck, the collision code spent about 3 ms every
   frame pushing it out of the object it rests in. Frozen wrecks are now skipped.
4. **On a shift your right hand goes to the gear lever**, for about half a second, as in GPL's cockpit. I checked this
   frame by frame against your gold video and its HUD gear readout.
5. **Low trackside objects are back.** Anything under 1 m tall used to be dropped. That removed Spa's hay bales and
   distance boards, WG's hay, the Ring's corner signs and Zandvoort's NO-NOX/Gulf boards. The cut is now 0.25 m.
6. **Monza's underpass approach is tree-lined again**, as in GPL. An old rule had removed those trees after they once
   drew as dark smears across the road. That bug is long fixed, and they now stand beside the road.
7. **Spa gets back a house, a banner and 124 trees and bushes.** The game's reader for GPL's track archives misread one
   entry in about 85, corrupting files at Spa. The DUNLOP SP banner by the start was drawn garbled; the white gabled
   house at Les Combes showed a stand-in; two kinds of tree and bush sprites never appeared. The Ring gets 7 back.
8. **Eau Rouge's FERODO board no longer shows its wooden back through the front** from some angles.
9. **Your /tmp no longer fills up.** Every race left an 11 MB copy of the track files in /tmp. On this PC there were
   1,127 of them, 3.7 GB. The game now cleans up after itself. The old ones can be deleted:
   `find /tmp -maxdepth 1 -name 'jl_*' -mmin +60 -exec rm -rf {} +` (a reboot also clears them).
