## What's fixed

**Control lag at the race start (PERF-4)** — the "lag between control inputs and car response, especially at the start".
- **Cause, measured:** Julia compiled code the first time it ran, in the middle of the race. Your first contact with another car in the crowded start froze the game for ~145 ms; a crash froze it for ~250 ms.
- That code is now compiled while the track loads (+0.7 s of loading); the race-start freezes are gone.
- Garbage per frame was halved, so the small memory-cleanup hitches now come half as often.
- The graphics driver may keep at most one frame queued. It wasn't building a queue in my windowed tests, but fullscreen may differ.
- Every session log now has a line for each hitch, saying what caused it.

**Cockpit (COCKPIT-2, DIALS-1)**
- In replay, the wheel, gloves, needles and HUD follow the recording, and the mirrors are live.
- The gear lever stands in GPL's gate, moves through the H pattern with your gear, and is drawn as dark wood instead of translucent orange.
- All six dials work: oil pressure and temperature, water, fuel pressure, volts, rev counter. Values follow GPL's own readout: pressures 79 psi while running, temperatures warming from 43 °C cold.
- The see-through wrists are closed: each sleeve now runs into its glove.

**Spa (SPATD-1)**
- **The "earthquake" jolts** were one-frame collision kicks at the road edges, from two causes:
  - A hedge grazed at speed kicked like a wall. Soft scenery is now capped at a small kick per frame, but still stops you if you plough in.
  - GPL's right wall stood **on the centreline** at Burnenville: our wall alignment had centred itself on a junction, not the road. That wall was also **your invisible barrier on the downhill left toward Burnenville**. Fixed: invisible contacts there 89 → 0.
- **AI pace:** at 70% they lapped at ~60% of the asked-for speed (Spa: 477 s against a 286 s target). The pace is now calibrated at load: the fastest AI lapped at 282 s equivalent, 240 km/h on the straights. This applies to every track.
- **AI off the road:** their racing line was placed in our world by one rigid fit, which missed our road on 9% of Spa. It now follows our drawn road. In a race, AI frames off the road went 8.8% → 0% everywhere past the grid, Eau Rouge included.
- Every session now logs each jolt with the object or wall that caused it.

**Nürburgring (RINGTD-1)**
- The 90° roll beside the tower before the North hairpin and the bucking under the post-Karussell banner were one bug. The body's drawn tilt read those structures as ground. I proved it on your recorded line; both are fixed. The physics car never rolled.

**Replays (REPLAY-4, REPLAY-5)**
- **R** now starts a new session, with its own replay and `.ibt`.
- At the end the replay **pauses** instead of sticking. **Space** replays it from the start, **Home/End** jump to the start/end, **PgUp/PgDn** jump ±10 s.

**Watkins Glen analyser (WGTD-1)**
- **The diagonal line across the map** was a lap that ran across a restart. You pressed R before anyone finished lap 1, so the lap counters never changed. A teleport now ends the session in the analyser, which also removes the bogus 2:17–2:26 lap times in that replay.
- **Lap list filter:** AI laps are hidden by default and unfinished laps are on a switch. The window opens on your own best lap against your second best.
- **"AI go a little off the track after the 90"** doesn't reproduce with tonight's AI. Not one AI car's edge left the road in 60,000 car-frames. Please look again at your next Watkins Glen race.
