## What you will feel (vs. before the epic)
- **Front end:** free front hubs and refitted tyres give the gold's balance and limit on both setups.
- **Steering weight:** about 20 % heavier through ordinary corners, lightening as the front saturates, with no lag
  behind quick inputs, like iRacing's rim torque. If it's too heavy on the TX, use the FFB gain.
- **Part throttle at high revs:** less pull mid-corner and when balancing a slide in the upper rev range, as iRacing
  has it. Full throttle and lift-off are unchanged.

  the June file. `JM_DEFAULT_TEMPLATE=june` brings the old car back.

  about 10 ms of iRacing. `JM_DAMPER_LINEAR=1` reverts.

## Still different from iRacing (open)
1. **The rear at the limit (held slides, cornering drag).** At ≥ 1.05 g the gold's rear runs 1.3-1.7° more slip than
   the sim's, while the front matches. That one mechanism accounts for the wider stable drift and the 100-400 N of
   extra cornering drag.
   - A softer rear fits the steady state: score 11.7 → 10.9.
   - The open-loop replay rejects it (1.22 → 1.33).
   - The new closed-loop replay (S13, `JM_IRG_CLOSED=1`: a driver correcting yaw on top of your steering) finds the
     two rears indistinguishable: the steering correction needed differs by < 0.1°.
   - So the open-loop penalty is mostly divergence, not feel. Even in closed loop, though, the sim tracks the gold's
     yaw with about 4° less body slip in the default slides. The gold's drift angle remains the main gap, and S13
     works on it next.
2. **Hard launches:** about 20 % slow. iRacing's engine bogs to about 2,000 rpm at the clutch bite and the rears hook
   up; julia's engine stays at about 7,400 rpm and spins the rears. This needs data, see below.
3. **Crest landings:** the sim lands 0.2 g softer. Bump stops, accelerometer position, tyre rate and unsprung mass are
   ruled out. Parked.
