## What your tests found
1. **Free revs (test 1):** iRacing's engine alone has a crank inertia of 0.052 kg·m², a third of julia's 0.18. In gear
   the 0.18 is still right: the rest sits beyond the clutch. Its own drag is 12-20 N·m below the in-gear coast drag.
2. **Standing starts (test 2):** julia's clutch passed too little torque at mid engagement (172 N·m at 0.40, iRacing
   212). So julia's engine flared to 8,000 rpm and the rears spun to the limiter, where iRacing's bogs to ~1,600 and
   hooks up.
3. **iRacing has an anti-stall clutch.** In 81 % of all part-engaged rows your foot (ClutchRaw) is further out than the
   clutch's actual engagement. Standing in gear with the pedal released, iRacing keeps the clutch open and the engine
   idles; in a full-throttle launch it slips the clutch at ~0.40 to hold ~1,600 rpm. That's why you never stall it.
4. **Power drifts (test 3, all at Parabolica):** 13 held drifts at 4-6° body slip. With your steering, julia's car
   follows them with about 0.7-0.8° less rear slip, and needs about 10-15° more steering-wheel angle.
   The Watkins Glen laps can't be used for this yet: the replay road is flat and the Glen's corners are banked.
