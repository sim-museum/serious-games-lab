# STATUS 2026-10-15: PHYSFID-1, julia's car against your iRacing data

You asked for a fidelity pass on the default and loose setups, with julia feeling FF-heavy and inert next to iRacing.
The method: feed iRacing's own recorded inputs from your 261010-12 sessions (228-383 windows) into julia's car and compare
what each car does, window by window. The sprint log is in PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md, from "PHYSFID-1 retrospective" on.

## What changes in the game (committed, all 59 gates pass)
1. **Force feedback is iRacing's own law** (S1). Your iRacing wheel settings, read from the .ibt: 28.3 N·m = full force,
   linear, no damper, no smoothing, no centring spring.
   - julia's steering torque was already right: 0.92 of iRacing's on the same inputs.
   - But julia squeezed it through tanh(1.3 × torque / 8) and added a spring. On iRacing's inputs that sent your wheel a
     median 69 % of full force, above 80 % for 43 % of the time: "hard to turn the wheel".
   - iRacing sends a median 18 %, never above 80 %. julia now sends the same.
   - `JM_FFB_MODE=legacy` restores the old feel.
2. **The car is no longer inert** (S2). On the same inputs julia's car yawed only 0.90 of iRacing's: 0.96 in gentle
   corners, but 0.77 above 150 km/h even in gentle ones. That is linear-range understeer.
   - The cause was the front tyres' cornering stiffness. I tested five values and kept the best, 26 (was 23).
   - Yaw response is now 0.99-1.01 up to 0.75 g, 0.96 up to 1.25 g, and 0.88 above 150 km/h.
   - The overall fit to iRacing improved (1.0514 → 1.0373), and the stability tests pass on both setups.
   - `JM_PHYSFID_S2=0` restores the old tyre.
3. **julia's .ibt files now record the steering-wheel angle as iRacing does** (S4). They had recorded the road-wheel
   angle, about 10x smaller.

## Measured, not changed
- **Ruled out:** more front grip, softer front load response, front toe, tyre load sensitivity, drivetrain efficiency
  and yaw inertia. Each fixes one corner of the data and loses more elsewhere.
- **The loose setup (WW103) matches iRacing worse than the default.**
  - At full throttle julia's WW103 accelerates 5-8 % less than iRacing's, at every speed and rpm. The default setup is
    within 1-4 %.
  - It isn't mass or fuel (both 605 kg / 43 L), gearing (rpm per km/h within 0.5 %), wheelspin or rotating inertia.
  - Even at the same overall gear ratio, iRacing's WW103 puts about 6 % more drive down than its default.
  - The cause is something in the setup itself that the recorded sessions can't separate: the differential, the toe or
    the camber.
- **At the grip limit julia still turns less than iRacing** (0.75 of its yaw at 1.25-1.5 g). The data can't settle the
  front/rear grip split there.
