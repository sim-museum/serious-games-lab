weights, mass and balance), ride heights, camber, toe, bars, fuel, bias, gearing and the diff. The game's start-up log

## What your capture showed (the short version)
- Perch and packer move in 1/64-inch clicks. A bigger perch offset means less preload: that corner sinks and sheds
  weight. At the top of its range the spring goes slack, and iRacing allows it.
- Camber and front toe in the garage follow ride height. Camber moves about 0.026° per mm and toe about 0.016 mm per mm,
  so the camber and toe rows show a result, not just the knob. julia's tab does the same.
- Tyre pressure changes the corner weights. The 261010 session's right-side 179 kPa moved them by about 15 N.
- **Dampers (from your 0 / 12-click laps):** iRacing's damping rises in a straight line with each click. Bump goes from
  3000 to 5200 N·s/m at the front and 1300 to 2350 at the rear; rebound from 1500 to 2300 at the front and 300 to 525 at
  the rear. Your base runs at +4/+8 sit on those lines.
- One iRacing quirk: a screenshot taken right after a click can show the previous solve. Your LR 30 N/mm shot is really
  about 40 N/mm.
