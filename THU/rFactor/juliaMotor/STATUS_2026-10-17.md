## What you can do now
**Launcher → Garage tab.** iRacing's setup page for the Lotus 49 is in julia: TIRES, CHASSIS and DRIVETRAIN, with
iRacing's names, steps and limits.
- **Knobs:** spring perch offsets (in iRacing's 1/64-inch clicks), spring rates, packers, bump and rebound clicks, camber,
  toe, both anti-roll bars (diameter and arms), fuel, brake bias, final drive and gears, the diff.
- **Derived numbers:** they move as you turn a knob, the way iRacing's garage moves them. They are corner weights, ride
  heights, shock deflection ("x of y"), cross weight, static camber and toe, and each gear's top speed.
- **Reset to the session** puts every knob back to the base setup's values (the iRacing session's own numbers, to the
  digit). **Save as… / Load…** keep named setups.
- **Base setup:** the garage follows the setup chosen on the Race tab (iRacing default or WW103), and remembers your
  changes for each one.
- **Use this garage for the next race** is on by default. Untick it to race the base setup as it is.

When you start a race with a modified garage, julia builds the car from it. That covers springs, preloads (corner
weights, mass and balance), ride heights, camber, toe, bars, fuel, bias, gearing and the diff. The game's start-up log
lists the knobs you changed.

## What your capture showed (the short version)
- Perch and packer move in 1/64-inch clicks. A bigger perch offset means less preload: that corner sinks and sheds
  weight. At the top of its range the spring goes slack, and iRacing allows it.
- Camber and front toe in the garage follow ride height. Camber moves about 0.026° per mm and toe about 0.016 mm per mm,
  so the camber and toe rows show a result, not just the knob. julia's tab does the same.
- Tyre pressure changes the corner weights. The 261010 session's right-side 179 kPa moved them by about 15 N.
- **Dampers (from your 0 / 12-click laps):** iRacing's damping rises in a straight line with each click. Bump goes from
  3000 to 5200 N·s/m at the front and 1300 to 2350 at the rear; rebound from 1500 to 2300 at the front and 300 to 525 at
  the rear. Your base runs at +4/+8 sit on those lines.
- One iRacing quirk: a screenshot taken right after a click can show the previous solve. Your LR 30 N/mm shot is really
  about 40 N/mm.
