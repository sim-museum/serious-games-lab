World Champion Vladimir Kramnik's Chess Variant: capture anything / no castle

Castling is illegal, and you may capture your own pieces (anything but your king). All other rules — checks, en passant, promotion, checkmate — are normal. Your opponent always plays at full strength and opens with sound, mostly closed theory (the English, the Closed Sicilian) — never the same way twice in a row. But once he is up by more than two pawns he starts hunting for combinations that capture his own pieces — and the bigger his lead, the more material he flamboyantly hands back, so the game stays a game.
