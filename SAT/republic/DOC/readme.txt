1) Game Issues


Slide Tweaks


 

This is not documented in the manual, information is available however 
from the in game help

 

A Slide Tweak lets you change the meat of an action while it is in progress.  
If the action flashes in the ‘action tracker’ then a slide tweak is available 
for that action.  View the action and the Slide Tweak will be available for 
you to change.

 

Do not worry if you miss it, as your recruit will choose for you, but they 
may not make the best choice.  It is best to go in and make the selection 
yourself in critical actions.

 

The Slide Tweak is a slider that allows you to choose one of three things:

 

q       An amount of resource

q       An approach on a scale

q       A choice out of two or three options

 

 

Resource Slide Tweak

Changing the amount alters the threshold in the conversation game that follows.
  Let’s say that you slide it up to the maximum amount.  
If you are bribing someone, this reduces the threshold for the conversation.  
If you are trying to arrange for that person to give you resources, 
the threshold is raised, making the conversation game harder.

 

Approach Slide Tweak

This lets you tailor the action for the ideology of the district it is run in.  
Select, for example, where to pitch a Rally, to Hearts or to Minds, 
or some mixture of the two.  Different mixtures work better in different types 
of district so experiment to find out what works best.

 

Choice Slide Tweak

This also lets you tailor the action for the ideology of the district it is 
run in.  You choose, for example, what slogan to use on leaflets or what 
message goes on your graffiti.  Some choices are better suited to the district 
that you are running it in, so choose wisely and experiment to find the best 
results.

2) Technical Support:

For the support for all your games bought through GOG.com, 
please visit our Support page at http://www.gog.com/support.
If you're logged in, go directly to "Support" page, where you can see 
a list of all your Good Old Games, otherwise you can find the game you're 
looking for through the smart search. Choose the game that you have problem 
with, and see whether the solution isn't already posted. If not, go to the 
"Contact us" page, select "Technical issues with games" and hit "Continue" 
to send us a message describing your problem. Fill all the required fields 
with proper data - please enter as much details as you can, this will help 
us solve your problem faster. All your messages and our replies will appear 
on your "My Account" page.
